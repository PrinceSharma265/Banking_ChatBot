Types of Credit Cards

Classic Card
The Classic Card is designed for customers beginning their credit journey. It carries a credit limit ranging from Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 50,000, depending on income, credit profile, and repayment history. This card is suitable for regular retail spends, grocery shopping, fuel purchases, and utility bill payments.

Gold Card
The Gold Card is meant for customers with higher spending power and a stable income stream. The credit limit for this category ranges from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 2,00,000. Gold Card holders enjoy enhanced benefits, including better reward point accrual, higher insurance cover, and access to chosen dining and shopping offers.

Platinum Card
The Platinum Card is targeted at premium salaried individuals and business owners. Credit limits for Platinum Cards range from Rs. 2,00,000 to Rs. 10,00,000. These cards provide superior privileges such as airport lounge access, accelerated reward points on selected categories, and exclusive lifestyle and travel privileges.

Super Premium Card
The Super Premium Card is offered to high-net-worth customers with excellent credit records. This card carries a credit limit above Rs. 10,00,000. Super Premium Card holders receive the highest tier of benefits, including concierge services, complimentary travel insurance, premium dining offers, and dedicated customer service.

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum Age
The minimum age to apply for any credit card from the bank is 21 years. Applicants should be Indian residents and must have reached the minimum age at the time of application.

Minimum Annual Income
To qualify for the Classic Card, applicants must have a minimum annual income of Rs. 2,40,000. This income requirement can be met through salary, pension, or regular business receipts for self-employed individuals.

Gold Card Income Requirement
For a Gold Card, the minimum annual income requirement is Rs. 5,00,000. Applicants should demonstrate stable earnings, ideally through salary slips, bank statements, or audited financials for business owners.

Platinum Card Income Requirement
The Platinum Card requires a minimum annual income of Rs. 10,00,000. The bank assesses the applicant’s overall financial strength, including salary, business income, investments, and existing liabilities while determining eligibility.

Credit Score
A good credit score is essential for credit card approval. The bank prefers applicants with a credit score above 700. Applicants with higher scores are more likely to receive preferred card variants and higher credit limits.

Fees and Charges

Annual Fee
Each credit card type attracts an annual fee according to the card category. Classic Cards generally have the lowest annual fee, Gold Cards carry a moderate annual fee, Platinum Cards attract a higher fee, and Super Premium Cards have the highest annual fee commensurate with premium benefits.

Joining Fee
A one-time joining fee may be charged at the time of issuing the card. For Classic Cards, the joining fee is nominal. The fee increases for Gold, Platinum, and Super Premium Cards based on the benefits and services provided.

Late Payment Charges
Late payment charges are levied if the cardholder fails to pay the minimum amount due by the due date. These charges are assessed as a fixed amount plus interest on the overdue amount. Timely payments are encouraged to avoid escalation of fees and adverse credit impact.

Cash Advance Fee
A cash advance fee of 2.5% of the amount withdrawn is applicable when the cardholder uses the credit card to obtain cash from an ATM or bank branch. The bank also applies interest on the cash advance amount from the date of withdrawal until full repayment.

Foreign Transaction Fee
A foreign transaction fee of 3.5% is applicable on international transactions or transactions conducted in foreign currency. This fee covers currency conversion and cross-border processing charges.

Interest Rate on Outstanding
The interest rate on outstanding amounts ranges from 36% to 42% per annum. This rate is applied to any unpaid balance after the due date and on cash advances. Interest is calculated on a monthly basis and added to the outstanding balance.

Minimum Amount Due
The minimum amount due is typically 5% of the total outstanding balance or a fixed minimum amount, whichever is higher. The minimum amount due must be paid by the due date to keep the account current and avoid late payment fees.

Reward Points System

How Points Are Earned
Credit card reward points are earned on every eligible purchase. Generally, cardholders earn 1 reward point for every Rs. 100 spent on retail purchases. Certain categories may offer accelerated point accrual based on the card variant.

Bonus Points on Categories
The bank offers bonus points on select categories such as dining, travel, fuel, online shopping, and groceries. Gold and Platinum cardholders often receive higher bonus points on these categories compared to Classic cardholders. Super Premium Cards may receive special bonus points on international spends and premium lifestyle categories.

How to Redeem Points
Reward points can be redeemed through the bank’s rewards portal, mobile app, or customer service centre. Cardholders may convert points to vouchers, cash back, statement credit, air miles, or merchandise from partner catalogs. Redemption options and point values are detailed in the cardholder agreement.

Point Expiry Policy
Reward points have an expiry period. Points earned must be redeemed within the stipulated validity period, typically 24 months from the date of earning. Unused points after the expiry period are forfeited. The bank sends periodic reminders through SMS and email regarding point balances and upcoming expiries.

Credit Card Safety

OTP Based Transactions
The bank enforces OTP-based authentication for online and remote card transactions. One-time passwords are sent to the cardholder’s registered mobile number for secure verification. This adds a layer of safety for e-commerce and digital payments.

How to Block Card
If a cardholder suspects fraudulent activity, misplacement, or theft of the credit card, they should immediately contact the bank’s customer care helpline to block the card. The bank provides 24/7 emergency card blocking services. Cardholders may also block the card through the mobile banking app or net banking portal.

Dispute Resolution Process
If the cardholder identifies unauthorized transactions, they should report the dispute to the bank within the specified time frame, usually 90 days from the transaction date. The bank investigates disputes, freezes the contested amount, and provides a resolution within the regulatory timeline. Documentation such as transaction details, merchant receipts, and dispute forms may be required.

Zero Liability on Fraud
The bank offers zero liability protection on confirmed fraudulent transactions made after the cardholder has reported the loss or theft promptly. As long as the cardholder notifies the bank immediately and cooperates with the investigation, they are protected against unauthorized charges.

Credit Score Impact

How Credit Card Usage Affects CIBIL Score
Credit card behavior influences the CIBIL score significantly. Timely payment of EMIs and credit card dues reflects positively on the credit report. High outstanding balances, missed payments, and defaults can negatively affect the score.

Ideal Credit Utilization Ratio
The ideal credit utilization ratio is below 30% of the sanctioned credit limit. Maintaining low utilization indicates responsible credit behavior. For example, if the credit limit is Rs. 2,00,000, the cardholder should aim to keep the outstanding balance below Rs. 60,000.

Importance of Timely Payments
Timely payments are crucial for maintaining a healthy credit score. Paying at least the minimum amount due by the due date avoids late fees and prevents negative remarks on the credit report. Consistent on-time payments over several months can improve the credit score and increase prospects for higher credit limits and additional products in the future.

This guide is intended to help customers understand the bank’s credit card offerings, eligibility norms, fees, rewards, safety features, and the effect on credit health. Customers are advised to read the full terms and conditions and use credit responsibly.
